Privacy Policy

Calm Sprint · Effective date: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MysticPlate (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use Calm Sprint, an Android focus timer app. Calm Sprint does not require an account.

Information stored on your device

Calm Sprint stores your timer settings, active timer state, focus and break session history, and achievement progress in the app's private storage on your device. The app uses this information to run the timer and show your statistics and achievements. Our app code does not upload these records to a developer-operated server.

Android backup is enabled for the app. Depending on your device and backup settings, Android may back up and restore some app data through your device's backup service.

Information collected through Firebase

Calm Sprint includes Google Firebase services to understand app usage and diagnose problems:

  • Firebase Analytics may automatically collect app activity such as screen views and sessions, an app-instance identifier, the Android Advertising ID when available, and approximate location derived from a masked IP address.
  • Firebase Crashlytics may collect crash reports, stack traces, relevant app state, device information, and installation identifiers when diagnosing crashes and app stability.
  • Supporting Firebase services may generate an identifier for this app installation and collect technical information needed to provide those services.

We use this information to understand how Calm Sprint performs, identify errors, and improve the app. Firebase may process this information on our behalf and use subprocessors to provide its services. For details about Google's handling of Firebase data, see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Information you send to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request.

Permissions and notifications

Calm Sprint uses a foreground service to keep a focus or break timer running while the app is in the background. If you allow notifications, the app can display the active timer and notify you when a session ends. You can manage notification permission in Android settings.

Sharing and security

We use Google Firebase for analytics and crash reporting. Android's backup service may also handle app data if backup is enabled on your device. We do not sell your personal information. Firebase encrypts data it collects while the data is in transit. Locally stored app data is kept in Android's app-private storage.

Retention and deletion

Data stored by Calm Sprint on your device remains there until Android removes the app's data or you uninstall the app. Copies held by an Android backup service may follow that service's separate settings and retention rules. Firebase data is retained according to the applicable Firebase service settings and policies.

To ask about information associated with your use of Calm Sprint, contact us using the email below. Because the app has no user accounts, we may need additional information to identify a particular Firebase record, and in some cases we may be unable to link a record to you.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will show when the latest version took effect.
